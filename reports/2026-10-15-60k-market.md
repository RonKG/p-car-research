# $60k Porsche market — 15 October 2026

Update to the [4 October snapshot](2026-10-04-60k-market.md). The ladder medians are unchanged. This pass checks what the 4 October live cars did, the other 911 and Cayman hammers that closed since then, and what is live tonight. Raw rows are in [../data/2026-10-15-cars-and-bids.json](../data/2026-10-15-cars-and-bids.json).

The bid ceiling is still **$57,100**. That is $60,000 after the 5% Cars & Bids buyer fee. Tax, title, and shipping are extra.

## What changed

Shop the same cars. A clean **2014 Cayman S manual** sold at **$50,000** on 12 October, unmodified. That is the middle of the manual-S range from 4 October (median $54,750).

The cars that were already expensive finished expensive. A track-modified **2016 Cayman GTS** sold at **$64,574**, on the $65,000 GTS median. A 13k-mile **2019 Carrera S cab** sold at **$116,500**. A **2021 Carrera 4S cab** sold after the auction at **$135,000**.

The 44k-mile **2014 Cayman S PDK** that looked like the best live car bid to **$39,750** and did not meet reserve. It did not sell. The PDK S median is $38,250.
