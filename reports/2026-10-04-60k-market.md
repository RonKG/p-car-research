# $60k Porsche market — 4 October 2026

Snapshot of Cars & Bids sold prices for a 911 Carrera S or above, or a six-cylinder Cayman, against a $60,000 budget. Raw rows are in [../data/2026-10-04-cars-and-bids.json](../data/2026-10-04-cars-and-bids.json).

## Recommendation

Shop a **2014–2016 Cayman S** first. The median hammer on Cars & Bids is **$44,000**, and 19 of 21 recent sales were at or under a $57,100 bid. A manual is the one to hunt (median **$54,750**, 8 of 10 still inside the cap). A PDK is the easy buy (median **$38,250**, 11 of 11 inside the cap).

The base 981 Cayman, the 2.7 flat-6, is the same chassis with money left over. Median **$40,125**, and all 26 sales in this set were inside the cap. The dearest of them was $56,500.

A **997 Carrera S** is the 911 that can work if the hammer stays in the low-to-mid $50,000s. Coupes in this window median **$53,500**. Manual 997.1 coupes median **$57,500**, so a nice one is the edge of the budget. A clean **991 Carrera S coupe** medians **$72,250**. A 992 Carrera S in this set sold for **$125,000–$143,000**.

## Budget

The $60,000 figure is the winning bid plus the Cars & Bids buyer fee. The fee is 5% of the hammer, with a $250 minimum and a $7,500 cap ([Cars & Bids](https://carsandbids.com/what-is/)). A bid of **$57,100** plus 5% is $59,955.

Tax, title, and shipping are on top of that. Shipping for these cars is often $1,000–$2,000. On a $50,000 win the fee alone is $2,500.

Set the walk-away number before the last hour of the auction. A result that only shows “bid to” did not sell. Reserve-not-met auctions are common under the medians below.

## How to shop the four cars that fit

1. **981 Cayman S, 2014–2016.** [Search](https://carsandbids.com/search/porsche/981-cayman). Manuals in the high $40ks to mid $50ks, or PDK cars in the high $30ks to high $40ks. The 981 flat-6 does not use the earlier IMS bearing. Read the service file, check the clutch on a manual, and bid less when the photos show prior damage.
2. **981 Cayman 2.7.** Same search. Choose it when the reserve for maintenance matters more than the extra 50 horsepower.
3. **987.2 Cayman S, 2009–2012.** [Search](https://carsandbids.com/search/porsche/987-cayman). The model page was rate-limited during this pull, so the sold sample is only the three cars that also appeared on the all-Porsche feed: $23,250, $29,250, and $32,061. That is enough to treat a straight 987.2 S as inside the budget. Prefer 2009–2012 over 2006–2008. Keep a bid under $40k unless the car is a low-mile manual.
4. **997 Carrera S.** [Search](https://carsandbids.com/search/porsche/997-911). Coupe median $53,500. A 20k-mile 2007 manual sold at $74,000, which is the kind of car that leaves the budget. 997.2 PDK coupes in this sample median about $70,000 (4 sales). If the 911 is the point, bid a higher-mile 997.1 S or a cab, and pay for a PPI aimed at bore scoring on the 3.8, IMS paperwork, and over-rev reports.

## Method

Pulled from [Cars & Bids Porsche search](https://carsandbids.com/search/porsche) on 4 October 2026.

- 981 Cayman: about 90 of 107 closed auctions on the site, spanning 2022–2026. This is close to the full Cars & Bids archive for that car.
- 997: the latest 4 pages of 361 closed auctions, roughly the last 12–18 months.
- 991 and 996: the latest 3 pages (325 and 492 closed auctions on the site).
- 992 and 993: the latest page, enough to show they sit above $60k.
- 987 and 718 model pages did not return a full closed set. The site answered later requests with HTTP 429. 718 six-cylinder cars (GT4, GT4 RS, GTS 4.0) are above this budget on the live GT4 RS alone.

Hammers are the sold or sold-after price. Live bids are separate and were current on the morning of 4 October 2026. Transmission “auto” in the data file is PDK or Tiptronic. This pass does not include Bring a Trailer, dealer asking prices, or private sales.
