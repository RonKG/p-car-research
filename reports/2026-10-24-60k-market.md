# $60k Porsche market — 24 October 2026

Update to the [15 October snapshot](2026-10-15-60k-market.md). The ladder medians are unchanged. This pass checks what the 15 October live cars did, the other 911 and Cayman hammers that closed since then, and what is live tonight. Raw rows are in [../data/2026-10-24-cars-and-bids.json](../data/2026-10-24-cars-and-bids.json).

The bid ceiling is still **$57,100**. That is $60,000 after the 5% Cars & Bids buyer fee. Tax, title, and shipping are extra.

## What changed

Shop the same cars. No 981 Cayman and no 981 Cayman S closed since 15 October. The manual-S median is still **$54,750**. The PDK S median is still **$38,250**.

The cars that could fit the cap sold inside it, or they did not sell.

A 117k-mile **2008 Carrera S cab** sold at **$34,000** on 16 October. That was the best live 911 on 15 October, when the bid was $20,750. It finished under the $42,800 cab median. A 60k-mile **2008 Cayman S** manual sold at **$36,000** on 21 October, above the $25,000 987.1 S median and inside the cap. A 98k-mile **2008 Cayman** sold at **$19,000**, under the $24,250 base median.

The **2012 Carrera 4S cab** bid to **$50,074** and did not meet reserve. The bid was inside the cap. The car did not sell.

The TechArt **2012 Carrera S** is a 991.1: 7-speed manual, 400 hp, and it sits on the 991 model page. The 15 October note had it with the 997.2s. It bid to **$72,500**, on the $72,250 991.1 S median, and did not sell.

The cars already through the cap stayed there. A 55k-mile unmodified **2016 Cayman GT4** sold at **$82,500**, under the $86,000 floor from the 4 October set. A 3k-mile **2012 Cayman R** sold at **$117,000** with no reserve. The two earlier R sales were about $63,000, so this one does not reset that median. A 6,500-mile **2025 GTS 4.0** sold at **$113,000**, above the $102,000 top of that set.
