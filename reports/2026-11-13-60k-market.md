# $60k Porsche market — 13 November 2026

Update to the [24 October snapshot](2026-10-24-60k-market.md). The ladder medians are unchanged. This pass checks what the 24 October live cars did, the other 911 and Cayman hammers that closed since then, and what is live tonight. Raw rows are in [../data/2026-11-13-cars-and-bids.json](../data/2026-11-13-cars-and-bids.json).

The bid ceiling is still **$57,100**. That is $60,000 after the 5% Cars & Bids buyer fee. Tax, title, and shipping are extra.

## What changed

Shop the same cars. Still no 981 Cayman S. A 51k-mile **2014 Cayman** manual, the base 2.7, sold at **$44,000** on 10 November. Sport exhaust and the Premium package. That is a bit over the $40,125 median and inside the cap.

The 47k-mile **2008 Cayman S** that was the best live car on 24 October sold at **$33,000**. Unmodified, manual, above the $25,000 987.1 S median, inside the cap. The one-owner **2006 Cayman S** bid to **$20,500** and did not sell. The 158k-mile **2007 Cayman S** bid to **$12,800** and did not sell.

996 Carrera 4S cars are selling above the old $22–35k range and still inside the cap. The X51 manual from 24 October sold at **$44,996**. A 63k-mile Tiptronic coupe sold at **$45,500**. A 25k-mile manual cab sold at **$47,672**. An 82k-mile manual bid to **$38,000** and the reserve held.

An 89k-mile **2011 Carrera S** coupe, PDK, sold at **$57,000**. That hammer is $100 under the bid ceiling. With the fee it is $59,850.

The 61k-mile **2013 Carrera S** manual sold at **$82,000**, about $10,000 over the $72,250 991.1 S median. The flat-4 **2018 Cayman GTS** sold after at **$61,500**, just over the cap. A 3k-mile **2025 GTS 4.0** sold after at **$108,000**.
